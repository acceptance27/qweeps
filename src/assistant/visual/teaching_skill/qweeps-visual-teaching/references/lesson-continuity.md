# A lesson needs a reasoning chain

Review conceptual continuity separately from audio continuity. One recording,
shared colours, a persistent header or a common subject cannot establish why
one teaching beat follows another.

Choose one learner goal and a trackable case. At each boundary identify the
result the learner just reached, the question it leaves open, and why the next
representation helps answer that question. Put that connection in the spoken
explanation and in a short visible `continuity.bridge`; set `continuity.from` to
the preceding beat ID (null for the opening). The opening explains the purpose
of the case. Keep the case's meaning stable through changes of representation.
Speak the bridge verbatim at the start of the incoming passage. Keep the previous
completed diagram visible while the narrator connects it to the next question;
handoff after the measured final word of that bridge. Audio passage boundaries
are not automatically visual scene boundaries. Begin the next construction at
handoff so it does not finish invisibly behind the retained diagram. Rewinding
and selection during the bridge must reconstruct the diagram actually visible.

For example: a component list tells us what an app borrows; classifying IP tells
us which kind of protection may be relevant; neither alone settles whether the
intended use is permitted, which motivates examining an exception and stated
licence terms. This is an example of question dependencies, not a mandatory
sequence for other subjects. State the actual dependency for the new lesson.

A memory exercise should say what earlier distinction it helps retrieve and
why that recall will be useful next. A new case assumption must be introduced as
a supposition. Preserve all legal/scientific conditions; don't invent causation
or a prerequisite merely to make a smooth story.

Reject generic connectors such as "next", "now let's explore", "as we saw" or
"these are related" when the prior result and remaining need are absent. Read
each adjacent pair's narration without headings: can a novice explain why the
second passage is needed? Require a quoted spoken bridge in the whole-lesson
narrative review. Fix a failed transition before publishing; audio alignment and
per-beat factual/visual approval do not substitute for that review.
