# Supplementary 3Blue1Brown method studies

Studied at commit `306a1346a356c7a7b097875ceb51c5cd67d0a56c` in
[3b1b/videos](https://github.com/3b1b/videos). These are observations from source
scenes, not a claim that their final videos were watched here. They expand the
method repertoire at the user's request. The user's three primary references
remain the quality examples; the other ZIP lessons are still not approved.

## Concrete sample → grouping → abstract measure

[ReasonByRepresentativeSample, part1.py](https://github.com/3b1b/videos/blob/306a1346a356c7a7b097875ceb51c5cd67d0a56c/_2019/bayes/part1.py#L1479)
constructs population groups, increases the sample, encloses subsets in rectangles,
and relates those regions to likelihoods. Identity and grouping explain what the
area model counts. Transfer: before an infographic presents a ratio, taxonomy,
market segment, or competency grouping, show recognizable instances and the
membership rule. Do not invent sample frequencies for historical causes.

## Same relationship, different frame of reference

[ShowTwoPerspectives, footnote.py](https://github.com/3b1b/videos/blob/306a1346a356c7a7b097875ceb51c5cd67d0a56c/_2019/bayes/footnote.py#L130)
re-expresses a shared overlap through different partitions. The overlap keeps
its meaning while the containing space changes. Transfer: make “out of which
whole?” visible in proportions, or align the same business case from customer
and supplier perspectives. Distinguish a change in viewpoint from new evidence.

## Re-encode the same data instead of replacing the conclusion

[RescaleToLogarithmic, covid.py](https://github.com/3b1b/videos/blob/306a1346a356c7a7b097875ceb51c5cd67d0a56c/_2020/covid.py#L818)
moves the same data dots and grid positions to a logarithmic coordinate system,
then introduces its scale. Transfer: preserve the concrete data while changing
representation; orient the viewer with ticks/units so a scale change is not
mistaken for changed observations. Camera zoom alone does not do this.

## Reveal the missing level of explanation

[SubtltyOfGrowthFactorShift, covid.py](https://github.com/3b1b/videos/blob/306a1346a356c7a7b097875ceb51c5cd67d0a56c/_2020/covid.py#L1687)
connects totals, differences and growth factors in separate aligned columns.
Each new relation is derived from the preceding quantities. Transfer: when a
summary leaves confusion, expose the intermediate relation; do not add another
summary label. In qualitative subjects use actual examples/evidence, not
arbitrary numerical strength meters.

## Adaptation boundary

The repository uses ManimGL and custom dependencies; this project uses Manim CE.
Its README explicitly notes compatibility differences. Study teaching operations,
then implement original geometry with supported APIs. Do not vendor its scenes
as CE templates or assume a new MCP server will supply these teaching decisions.
The repository's scene content has a CC BY-NC-SA license; no scene source/assets
are copied into QWEEPS by this study.
